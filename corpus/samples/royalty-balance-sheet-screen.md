# Commodity Royalty and Balance-Sheet Risk Screen

Screening criteria for asset-light royalty businesses through commodity cycles:

- **Operating leverage protection** — revenue tied to top-line production volume without direct exposure to field capex inflation.
- **Counterparty concentration** — cap single-operator exposure below 20% of trailing twelve-month cash flow.
- **Liquidity cushion** — require undrawn revolver plus unrestricted cash to cover at least 24 months of fixed obligations under downside strip pricing.
- **Margin of safety** — model NAV at mid-cycle commodity prices rather than spot peaks.
