# Incremental ROIC and Capital Allocation Checklist

Evaluate management capital deployment across five competing uses of free cash flow:

1. **Organic reinvestment** — compare incremental return on invested capital (ROIC) over a 5-year rolling window against weighted average cost of capital.
2. **Bolt-on M&A** — audit historical acquisition write-downs and check whether acquired gross margin expanded or diluted core returns.
3. **Share repurchases** — verify buybacks reduced diluted share count net of stock-based compensation and occurred below intrinsic value estimates.
4. **Debt reduction** — stress-test net leverage at trough EBITDA margins and review maturity walls within 36 months.
5. **Dividends** — measure payout ratio against normalized maintenance capex rather than GAAP net income.
