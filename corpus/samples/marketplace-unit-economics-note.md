# Two-Sided Marketplace Unit Economics Framework

Key ratios for evaluating liquidity, take-rate durability, and cohort payback in two-sided networks:

- **Contribution margin per transaction** = Net take rate minus payment processing, insurance/trust costs, and variable support.
- **Supply utilization** — track active supply hours vs. fulfilled demand hours by metro density tier to separate supply-constrained from demand-constrained markets.
- **Incentive elasticity** — distinguish promotional spend that pulls forward existing demand from spend that permanently increases cohort retention past day 90.
- **Payback discipline** — compare blended CAC against 12-month gross profit per active buyer rather than gross bookings.
